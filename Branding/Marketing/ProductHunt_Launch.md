## Description (Markdown)
Hey Product Hunt! 👋

We all use online tools every day to compress images, generate QR codes, or format JSON. But have you ever stopped to think about what happens to your files when you upload them? 

Most "free" tools process your data on their servers. They might store it, mine it, or leave it vulnerable to hacks.

I built **NeedTools** to fix this. It’s a suite of 100% free web tools that run **entirely in your browser**.

🔒 **Zero-Upload Architecture:** We use WebAssembly and HTML5 Canvas. Your files never leave your device.
⚡️ **Lightning Fast:** No waiting for files to upload or download.
🚫 **No Limits or Watermarks:** Completely free, forever.

**Current Tools Include:**
- PDF Compressor & JPG Converter
- QR Code Generator (with custom logos)
- Image Compressor & Resizer
- Password Generator & Tester
- JSON Formatter & Minifier

I’d love to hear your feedback on the UI and performance! What other tools would you like to see added that process data locally?
