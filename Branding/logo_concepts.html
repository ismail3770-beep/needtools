Logo Concepts for NeedTools

Based on the letters 'N' and 'T'

Concept 1: The 'NT' Monogram

A clever combination. The left side forms the 'N', and the right side acts as the 'T'. It perfectly represents NeedTools.

Concept 2: Negative Space 'N'

A bold, app-icon style logo. It focuses purely on a modern, geometric 'N' cut out of the brand's primary blue color.

Concept 3: The Folded 'N'

A highly modern, tech-focused 'N'. It uses gradients to create a 3D folded ribbon effect, symbolizing layers of tools.
